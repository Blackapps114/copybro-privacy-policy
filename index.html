2. Information Stored by CopyBro

CopyBro allows you to save copied text on your Android device and access it later.

We do not have access to the texts you save in the application.

The following features operate locally on your device:

  • Saving copied text

  • Marking text as a favorite
  • Deleting saved text

CopyBro may use Android notifications to inform you that a copied text can be saved.

Notifications are used solely to provide this functionality within the application.

The texts you save in CopyBro are not transmitted to our servers. We do not access, collect, or analyze the content of your saved texts for advertising or other purposes.

7. Internet Connection

CopyBro may require an internet connection to display advertisements and to provide services supplied by third-party providers.
